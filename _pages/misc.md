Below is a list of study materials and other miscellaneous items that I find useful, interesting and/or impactful to my own learning and growth. I will occasionally update this page. 

Videos (mostly serious work stuff)
-----
1. Some of the most beautiful and intuitive explanations of mathematics and puzzles: [3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown)
2. Lecture series on mathematical fundamentals and some advanced topics: [Bright Side of Mathematics](https://www.youtube.com/@brightsideofmaths) and [MathDoctorBob](https://www.youtube.com/@MathDoctorBob/)
3. Luis Seco's lectures on [mathematical finance](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZqGKBeV8wtaXGYM0NS9iTRzUhfR4Vqin)
4. Steve Brunton's [control theory fundamentals](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMrJAkhIeNNR20Mz-VpzgfQs5zrYi085m)
5. Brian Douglas's great [control theory introduction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lBC1nEq0_nk&list=LL&index=112&t=734s&ab_channel=MATLAB) and [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@BrianBDouglas)
6. Stephen Boyd's lectures on [convex optimization](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rMJqxxviPa4AmDClvcbHi6h) 
7. Andrew D. Lewis's lectures on [signals and systems](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLbPr5CUeh0DrOK_3jKxSI-VS2tUvDS9vU)
8. Los Alamos National Laboratory lecture series on [convex relaxation of optimal power flow](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLeuOzWTGxj2ZZ_XUutDwNFvNfSWwWCgR5)  

Videos (others)

2. Film music analysis: [Shrek Trilogy*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7YCTJxfcA4&ab_channel=Sideways) and [Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ozbKHKntpCc&ab_channel=Sideways) 
3. J. Kenji Lopez-Alt's baking powder trick for [roast potatoes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=argKpeiKFfo&ab_channel=J.KenjiL%C3%B3pez-Alt) and [chicken wings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptxrb2k7Y-s&ab_channel=J.KenjiL%C3%B3pez-Alt)
